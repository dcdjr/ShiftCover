# User Research Observations

Our compilation of user research & analysis from our ShiftCover survey.

## Observation 1: Shift Coverage Is a Recurring Problem
- Observation Statement: Needing shift coverage is a common problem among shift-based employees, with many needing coverage multiple times per month.
- Supporting Data: 56 out of 72 respondents (77.8%) reported needing coverage at least once per month, while 37 respondents (51.4%) needed coverage two or more times per month.
- Team Action: Prioritize making the process of requesting and finding shift coverage as simple and efficient as possible.


## Observation 2: Employees Rely on Multiple Communication Methods
- Observation Statement: Many employees rely on informal communication methods to find shift coverage, which can make the process inefficient and frustrating.
- Supporting Data: 46 out of 72 respondents (63.9%) use group chats, 40 (55.6%) ask coworkers in person, and 28 (38.9%) contact their managers directly. Several respondents described having to contact multiple coworkers before finding coverage.
- Team Action: Provide a centralized platform where employees can post available shifts and communicate coverage requests without contacting coworkers individually.


## Observation 3: Employees Prioritize Basic Shift Management Features
- Observation Statement: Employees are more interested in practical shift management features than advanced functionality like AI-generated shift posts.
- Supporting Data: 54 out of 72 respondents (75%) selected posting shifts for coworkers to claim, while 53 (73.6%) selected notifications for available shifts. Only 5 respondents (6.9%) selected AI-generated shift posts.
- Team Action: Prioritize shift posting, claiming, and notifications in the initial version of ShiftCover. Defer AI features until the core functionality is implemented.


## Observation 4: There Is Interest in Using ShiftCover
- Observation Statement: A majority of respondents expressed interest in using ShiftCover if their workplace offered it for free, suggesting potential demand for the application.
- Supporting Data: 41 out of 72 respondents (56.9%) rated their likelihood of using ShiftCover as a 4 or 5 out of 5. Another 17 respondents (23.6%) selected 3 out of 5.
- Team Action: Focus on delivering a functional, free initial version of ShiftCover and gathering feedback from actual users before committing to a monetization plan.
